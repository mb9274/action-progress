## How we work

We work through **advocacy**, **donation** and **sensitization**. Our two focus areas are girl child education and the child and maternal death rate.

- **Advocacy:** speaking up for less privileged girls, mothers and children.
- **Donation:** working with supporters to provide practical help.
- **Sensitization:** raising community awareness of the issues affecting the people we serve.

## Where we work

Action for Progress is based in Brookfields, west end of Freetown, Sierra Leone. Our work is rooted in this community.
