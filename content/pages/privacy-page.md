## Who we are

Action for Progress is a charitable organization based in Brookfields, west end of Freetown, Sierra Leone.

## What this website collects

This is a static website. It does not create accounts, process payments, or run advertising trackers.

When you use a form on this site, we receive the details you choose to send, such as your name, email address and message. We use them only to reply to your enquiry or to follow up on your offer of help.

## Children's privacy

We do not publish full names, exact locations, school names, or other identifying details of children without written permission from a parent or guardian.

Photographs and stories involving children are published only after written guardian consent has been recorded. If you believe a photograph or story should not be on this site, contact us and we will review it promptly.

## Sharing buttons

Stories include share buttons for Facebook and WhatsApp. When you use them, you leave this website and the privacy policy of that service applies.
